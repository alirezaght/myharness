## Decision

Use a frozen paired benchmark, predeclared per-proposal improvement threshold, no material acceptance regression, zero demonstrated approval/isolation/privacy bypass, independent review and explicit activation approval. Missing or contaminated evidence cannot pass.

## Rationale

Current byte accounting and AL simulations do not establish general engineering-quality improvements.

## Impact

Evaluation thresholds must be fixed before results; canary stop conditions and rollback retain exact version provenance.
