## Decision

Automatically collect incidents and draft proposals; require explicit developer approval for every activation initially. Security, authority and approval changes always require explicit human approval.

## Rationale

Failure evidence creates hypotheses rather than authority; existing spec and decision gates remain authoritative.

## Impact

No automatic self-modification or activation; later low-risk automation requires separate evaluated approval.
