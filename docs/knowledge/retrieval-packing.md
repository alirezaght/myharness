# Bounded relationship evidence selection

Lexical anchors now lead to bounded typed relationship expansion before the output
allowance is filled. Reverse dependency paths receive priority over repeated
incidental assertions from those anchors. Within that priority, shorter paths and
stable assertion IDs determine order. Typed expansion visits each entity once. After traversal,
scalar facts on reached entities (including decision content and design rationale)
can use the remaining examined-work allowance. Scalar facts retain their own
citations and never become graph edges or traversal roots. Typed paths keep packing
priority over those explanatory facts.

Packing admits a path together with its supporting assertions, citations and
required derivation parents. If the remaining assertion or byte allowance cannot
hold that unit, it is withheld as a unit. Byte compaction removes the most recently
admitted unit, preserving earlier supporting evidence. Exclusions and truncation
remain visible; a missing consumer never proves absence of a dependency. Source
partitioning, citation revalidation, revocation checks and existing global limits
continue to apply.

Public synthetic refresh/query regressions cover saturated lexical matches,
graph-only consumers, two-hop paths, duplicate imports, cycles, byte pressure,
task-private neighbors, reconnects, extraction insertion order, and stale, revoked
or purged evidence. Stable decision-ID and task-linked design queries also assert
actual content, status and rationale values. These tests establish the bounded retrieval behavior. They do
not establish improved private benchmark scores or complete the Knowledge mission.
Prior failed evaluation evidence remains unchanged.

The permitted candidate pool still has a 2,000 assertion cap applied before lexical
relevance ranking, and expansion starts from at most the requested assertion count
of lexical seeds. Extremely broad matches can therefore miss an anchor before
packing. Unsupported languages and dynamic references retain their existing
coverage limitations; this change creates no inferred dependency edges.
